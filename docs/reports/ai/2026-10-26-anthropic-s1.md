# Anthropic S-1: first public financial disclosure

**Filed:** 2026-10-26. **Registrant:** Anthropic, PBC (CIK 2130357). **Filing:** [SEC Form S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm).

Anthropic has filed for a proposed IPO but is **not yet publicly traded**. Nasdaq symbol `ANT` is proposed; price, share count, and listing date are not final. Form S-1 is not a Form 10-Q or 10-K, so the primary 10-Q/10-K fact collector continues to treat Anthropic as outside its quarterly coverage. The reported figures below have been added as **primary-regulatory actuals** to `data/financial_db/manual_observations.json`, with the S-1 URL, document form, accounting basis, period, scope, and original scale recorded on each row.

## Data limits

- Annual and six-month flows are kept as separate observation periods, not mixed with quarterly figures or annualized run-rate revenue.
- Only financial concepts already supported by the v3 metric catalog were mapped. Convertible notes and redeemable preferred stock were not relabeled as `total_debt` or ordinary equity.
- The proposed ticker `ANT` is **not** set as a live trading ticker until a listing is confirmed.
- The S-1 registration statement has not become effective. Future amendments may revise the filing.
