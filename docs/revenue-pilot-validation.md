# Paid pilot demand validation

Goal: validate willingness to pay before expanding the research product.

A submitted public GitHub issue is an expression of interest, not a purchase or revenue. Track unique non-bot participants, use cases, budget ranges, observation period, and missing measurements separately. Never collect private personal or financial information in public issues. Preserve primary-source evidence, data licenses, and financial data integrity. Do not claim conversion rates without a measured denominator.

Pilot form submissions are public and are not paid orders. Evaluate whether the GitHub-login requirement biases respondents before treating survey results as representative.
